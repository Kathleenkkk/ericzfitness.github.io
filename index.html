A more thoughtful daily fitness coach.

Eric Z Fitness Coach is a privately developed fitness companion being tested for personalized workout suggestions, meal planning, and progress tracking.

What we're building

Planned integrations include Oura sleep and readiness insights and Apple Health weight and body-composition data, with user permission. Workout video analysis is also under development.

Some features are prototypes or not yet available. This site provides information for testing and third-party integration registration; it does not offer public account registration.

Important

Fitness suggestions are informational and are not medical advice. The app is being developed for private testing, not public distribution.
