Privacy Policy

Eric Z Fitness Coach ("the App") is a private fitness-coaching project under development. This policy describes the data we intend to process during authorized testing. Features and integrations described below may not yet be active.

1. Information collected

Depending on the features used, the App may process workout entries, meal logs, fitness goals, progress measurements, and videos or photos you choose to upload. If you connect Oura, we may access the specific data categories you authorize, such as sleep, daily readiness and activity. If you grant Apple Health permission in a future native build, the App may read selected measurements such as weight and body-fat percentage.

2. How information is used

Authorized data is used to display fitness trends, provide workout and recovery suggestions, and support features you request. AI-powered features may transmit the information needed for your request to a third-party AI service provider, including Anthropic, for processing. We will not intentionally send complete health histories when a smaller set of information is sufficient.

3. Access and sharing

Access is intended to be limited to the person testing the App and the developer operating the test backend. Data is not sold to advertisers. Data may be processed by infrastructure and AI providers as necessary to deliver a requested feature. Do not connect another person's account without their permission.

4. Storage and security

The App is under development. Data may be stored locally on the device and/or on the private development backend, depending on the feature. Access tokens and credentials must be kept on the backend rather than embedded in the mobile App. Security measures and retention rules will be reviewed before broader deployment. Do not upload sensitive material you are uncomfortable sharing during testing.

5. Your choices

Connecting Oura and granting Apple Health permissions are optional. You can revoke Oura access through your Oura account's authorized applications settings and Apple Health permissions through iOS settings. To request deletion of test data, contact the developer using the contact address listed on the Oura application registration. Revoking access stops future authorized collection but does not automatically delete previously stored data.

6. Children's data

The App is intended for adult private testers, not children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as features, hosting, and data handling change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the application developer using the contact email provided in the Oura developer registration.
